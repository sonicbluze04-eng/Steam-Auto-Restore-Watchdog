========================================================================
   STEAM AUTO-RESTORE WATCHDOG v1.3 - USER MANUAL & TUTORIAL
========================================================================

Thank you for downloading the Steam Auto-Restore Watchdog! 

This lightweight, automated tool runs silently inside your Windows System 
Tray. It continuously maintains a local "Time Capsule" backup, tracks 
your profile states, and automatically pushes missing achievements BACK 
to Valve's live servers if a cloud sync glitch or network error ever 
deletes them.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
🚀 SECTION 1: HOW TO START THE APPLICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Make sure your official Steam Desktop client is running and logged in.
2. Double-click 'steam_tool.exe'.
3. The app runs as a hidden background utility, so NO windows will open.
4. Look at the bottom-right corner of your desktop taskbar (by the clock).
5. Click the small arrow (▲) to show your hidden tray icons.
6. Look for the custom Blue Square Icon. Your watchdog is active!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚙️ SECTION 2: THE SYSTEM TRAY OPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Right-click the Blue Square Icon to control your app:

🔹 1. "Backup Right Now"
   - Manually scans your local Steam files and updates your master save file 
     named 'offline_achievements_backup.json' inside your Documents folder.
   - Anti-Clutter Filter: If you haven't unlocked anything new, the app 
     will notify you and skip creating identical copies to keep your system clean.

🔹 2. "Configure Sync List..." (New in v1.3)
   - Left-click this to pop open a sleek, dark-themed game checklist window.
   - Here, you can check or uncheck individual games to choose exactly 
     which profiles the software is allowed to backup and monitor.
   - Click "Save Configurations" when you are done to apply your filters!

🔹 3. "Enable Auto Mode" (Toggle Switch)
   - Left-click this option to check or uncheck background automated loops.
   - Checked (ON): The app wakes up every 10 minutes to scan for missing data.
   - Unchecked (OFF): Background tasks pause completely.

🔹 4. "Exit Tool"
   - Shuts down the background monitoring process and removes the tray icon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
🛡️ SECTION 3: HOW AUTOMATED RESTORATION WORKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This app is entirely self-healing! While Auto Mode is active:
1. Every 10 minutes, the script compares your active local Steam directory 
   against your trusted historical backup file inside your Documents folder.
2. It completely bypasses any games you unchecked in the "Configure Sync List" UI.
3. If it detects an enabled game profile missing any milestones that you previously 
   earned, the tool hooks directly into your running local Steam Client engine.
4. It initializes a Steamworks API socket bridge, sets the target Game App ID, 
   and executes a direct "SetAchievement" sync command.
5. This pushes the lost data back onto Valve's live databases automatically.
6. Once completed, a native Windows Notification Banner will pop out from 
   the edge of your screen informing you of the successful auto-restore!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
📂 SECTION 4: WHERE ARE MY FILES?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application keeps your system perfectly clean by containing all data 
inside a dedicated workspace directory:
📂 C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Documents\SteamWatchdog\

- sync_config.json                 -> Saves your checked/unchecked UI game selections.
- offline_achievements_backup.json  -> Your actual encrypted backup database.
